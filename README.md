# [(Readable) Ossining Recycling & Sanitation Schedule](https://www.ossining.info)

In 2023, I redesigned the sanitation and recycling calendar so that I could more easily use it. I wanted to address a few things about [the official calendar](https://www.villageofossiningny.gov/public-works/pages/sanitation-recycling):

- Depending on the street on which you live, you will have one of two schedules. But these two schedules are combined into one document, even as the calendars are mailed to our homes.
- There is a list of holidays occupying space on the page, but this information is redundant as the calendar will visibly show, for instance, no trash collection on a holiday.
- On the day when one is most likely to have their blue recycling bin at the curb, the calendar marks the date in red. On the day when one is most likely to have their green recycling bin at the curb, the calendar marks the date in blue.
- Bulk (non-metal, non-electronic) collection and yard waste collection are not indicated on the calendar. They are instead explained in text above the calendar.
- Weekends are shown, but they are never needed.

Now, the work is packaged for anyone who could want it. At [ossining.info](https://www.ossining.info), you are free to:
- Download/print the current calendar
- Subscribe to a calendar feed
